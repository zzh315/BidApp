Guidance only, from the numbers you enter. Stamp duty uses the Victorian first-home-buyer principal-place-of-residence scale. Check anything that decides a bid with your conveyancer.

Every money field is in thousands. Type 670 for $670,000 and 1 for a $1,000 increment. Decimals are fine — 7.3 means $7,300.

The auction

Your guess at the vendor's reserve. Below it the property cannot sell, so the goal is to be top bidder cheaply for the pass-in. Leave 0 if you have no read.

Target is the number you want to win at. Hard stop is the number you will not cross. Set them equal if you only want one limit.

The money

Shared-equity share of the purchase price, e.g. 30 for Help to Buy on an existing home. Enter 0 if you are not using a scheme.

Count the lender's mortgage plus any second mortgage a shared-equity scheme registers over the title.
